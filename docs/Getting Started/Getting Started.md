# Getting started

## What is Hack Club?
Hack Club is a global, 501(c)(3) nonprofit network of high school computer science clubs and young programmers. Founded in 2014 by Zach Latta, it operates on a "learn-by-doing" philosophy, providing teens with the tools, grants, and online communities to build their own software and hardware projects.

## How to join Hack Club Slack?
Visit hackclub.com, enter your email and follow the steps.

Alchemize is a YSWS event(online) organised by the community members of Hack Club.
